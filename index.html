    Case studies

    Selected work, written as challenge, solution and impact.

    Pairwise video evaluation for RLHF (Turing)

    Challenge

    AI-generated video pairs were often near-identical, so preference rankings needed a defensible reason behind every call.

    Solution

    Compared pairs on prompt adherence, physical plausibility, motion and temporal consistency, applying cinematography knowledge of framing, lighting, depth of field and pacing. Wrote a rationale for each verdict.

    Impact

    Kept a 98%+ accuracy rating across reviewed batches and caught artifacts earlier review passes missed.

    Root-cause analysis on conversational AI quality (TELUS International)

    Challenge

    Distributed teams produced inconsistent evaluations, and recurring errors were not being traced to their source.

    Solution

    Ran root-cause and gap analyses, tracked quality metrics across batches and triaged issues by escalation path, separating merchant-owned from platform-owned problems.

    Impact

    Contributed to a 20% improvement in workflow and dataset quality and to refined business guidelines.

    Robot-arm video annotation (Micro1)

    Challenge

    Robot-learning datasets need exact action boundaries and reliable labels for failed attempts.

    Solution

    Segmented each action, event and error, classified failure modes such as missed grasps and dropped objects, and escalated ambiguous cases to project managers.

    Impact

    Delivered consistent ground-truth data and guideline improvements that reduced ambiguity for the wider team.

    QA workflow automation with n8n

    Challenge

    Manual task routing, reporting and review prioritization slowed evaluation work.

    Solution

    Built n8n pipelines using APIs and webhooks for routing, reporting and QA notifications, plus an AI-assisted edge-case flagging workflow.

    Impact

    Less manual effort, and reviewers spend their time on the cases that need them.
